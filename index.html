A few things
worth knowing.

How much does a music video cost?

Our focus is affordable production for independent artists. Pricing is quoted around your song and creative requirements. The agreed scope includes three concepts, three revision rounds, and promotional clips. Additional work is quoted before it proceeds.

How quickly can you deliver?

Fast production is central to our approach. Your actual schedule will be agreed after we review the track, creative direction, and release needs. No delivery date is confirmed until the project is scoped.

Can you work with my footage or show me in the video?

Hybrid videos can combine your footage with AI-generated visuals. Tell us whether appearing on screen is important; artist likeness and performance requirements will be reviewed as part of the concept and project agreement.

What files will I receive?

We deliver in industry-standard resolution and aspect ratios appropriate to the agreed release plan. Exact formats and promotional-clip specifications are confirmed in the project scope.

Who owns the finished content?

At project completion and final payment, the client receives the digital rights to the finished content that Loud Light Films owns and can transfer. The project agreement defines the transfer and identifies any third-party licenses or limits applicable to AI-generated material.

You must have permission to use music, images, footage, and likenesses supplied for the project.

Do you work with record labels?

Yes. Independent artists come first, and labels, managers, and artist teams are welcome to discuss individual releases or multiple projects.
